ClipMark Support

ClipMark is a macOS menu bar clipboard manager with screenshot capture and lightweight annotation tools.

Contact

For support, feature requests, or bug reports, email tsibatov@yahoo.com.

Common Help

If area capture does not work, open System Settings > Privacy & Security > Screen Recording and make sure ClipMark is enabled.

If the menu bar icon is hidden, use the macOS Control Center menu bar settings or quit other menu bar apps to make room.

Privacy

ClipMark stores clipboard history and screenshots locally on your Mac. Read the Privacy Policy.
