Malina privacy policy

Updated 24 September 2026.

Your writing stays on your Mac

Malina does not collect or transmit your documents, images, identifiers, usage information or analytics to its developer. There are no accounts, advertising, trackers or network services in the editor.

Local storage

Your documents remain in files you choose. Settings, file permission bookmarks, open-tab snapshots and recovery copies are stored locally in Malina’s application data folder. They are not uploaded by Malina. A folder you independently synchronize with another service remains subject to that service’s policy.

Images and permissions

Images you insert are stored beside the document in an images folder. Malina requests access only to files and folders you select. Clipboard contents are used when you paste or when the app checks whether Paste is available; they are not transmitted. Remote images are not downloaded.

Retention and deletion

Recovery copies may remain after you close a document so that accidental edits can be recovered. You can open the recovery folder from File → Local Recovery. To remove all local app data, quit Malina and remove its application data using Finder. Removing app data does not delete documents saved elsewhere. Images remain on disk after Undo to support Redo; remove unused images yourself when they are no longer needed.

Apple and external links

Apple processes App Store purchases and may process system diagnostics according to your Apple settings and Apple’s privacy policy. Malina contains no payment or receipt-validation service. Links you deliberately open are handled by your chosen browser and the destination website’s policy.

This policy is available offline from Help → Privacy Policy.

Contact

For support or privacy questions, email tsibatov@yahoo.com. Please do not include private documents unless they are needed to explain your request.
