ClipMark Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 12, 2026

Data Collection

ClipMark does not collect, sell, or share personal data.

Local Storage

Clipboard history and screenshots are stored locally on your Mac. Full-size screenshots are saved on disk, and compact thumbnails are used in the app history.

Screen Recording Permission

ClipMark uses macOS Screen Recording permission only when you start screenshot capture. Captures are initiated by you and processed locally on your Mac.

Support Emails

If you contact support, we may receive the information you choose to include in your message. Support requests can be sent to tsibatov@yahoo.com.

Contact

For privacy questions, email tsibatov@yahoo.com.
