QuickLinks / Clipmark Support

Help for iPhone and iPad.

QuickLinks - Bookmarks & Tasks, displayed as Clipmark on your device, is a local-first workspace for saving links, folders, tasks, and notes.

Contact support

Email tsibatov@yahoo.com with your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, app version, and a description of the problem. Please avoid including private links or notes unless needed to explain the issue.

Getting started

Choose Start fresh during onboarding for an empty workspace, or try the optional demo content. Use + to add a link, folder, task, or note. Global Search finds items across all four sections.

Your data

Your records are stored locally on your device. Reset All Data in Settings permanently removes them from the app.
