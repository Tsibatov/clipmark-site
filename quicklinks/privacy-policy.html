QuickLinks / Clipmark Privacy Policy

For iPhone and iPad. Last updated: September 8, 2026

This policy covers QuickLinks - Bookmarks & Tasks, displayed as Clipmark on your device.

Clipmark does not collect, track, sell, or share personal data with the developer.

Data Handling

Your links, folders, notes, and tasks are stored locally on your device. Clipmark does not include analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, third-party trackers, or developer-operated servers.

Permissions

Clipmark does not require developer account registration and does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, or photo library access.

Contact

If you contact support, the developer receives the information you choose to include in your email and uses it to respond to your request.

For support or privacy questions, email tsibatov@yahoo.com.
